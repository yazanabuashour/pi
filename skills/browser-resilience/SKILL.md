# Continue when browser access is blocked

When a challenge blocks the task, stop repeating waits, clicks, or navigation.
Try an official API, export, feed, or downloadable data. For factual research,
switch to another source and disclose the substitution. Use `web_search` and
`fetch_content` instead of search-engine forms. Distinguish search-index claims
from live verification. Accessible content can still be wrong or stale.

Do not solve or outsource interactive challenges. Do not rotate identities to
evade limits, borrow another account, or exceed the user's authorization. The
user may complete a challenge manually where the service allows it. Respect
HTTP 429 responses. If access remains blocked, report what you could not verify.

Do not assume that profiles, headed mode, proxies, or cloud providers guarantee
access. Use the upstream `agent-browser` skill for browser operations.
Do not expose private services or copy session credentials to gain access.
